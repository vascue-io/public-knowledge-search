We, Vascue Limited (“Vascue”, “Company”, “we”, “us”, “our”) are the owners of the website www.vascue.io, the WhatsApp Assistant and AI Claims Assistance (collectively the “Platform”).

The WhatsApp Assistant is an AI-powered tool that automates patient interactions via WhatsApp, handling tasks like clinic introductions, appointment scheduling, service inquiries, and reminders, with multilingual support and integration with systems like Google Calendar.

The AI Claims Assistant is a solution designed to automate insurance claim processes and managing complaints based on system data, aiming to reduce administrative workload and errors.

## Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Platform and store certain information. Tracking technologies used are beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Platform. The technologies We use may include:

-   Cookies or Browser Cookies. A cookie is a small file placed on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept Cookies, you may not be able to use some parts of our Platform. Unless you have adjusted your browser setting so that it will refuse Cookies, our Platform may use Cookies.
-   Web Beacons. Certain sections of our Platform and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs) that permit Vascue, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and for other related website statistics (for example, recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be “Persistent” or “Session” Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser. We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

-   Strictly Necessary Cookies. Set by us and required for the Platform to function, including security, load balancing, and remembering your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off.
-   Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics). Set by Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to help us understand how visitors use the Platform (such as pages visited, approximate location, and device type) so we can improve it. IP addresses are anonymized. These load only if you grant the “Analytics” category in our cookie banner.
-   Marketing Cookies (Meta Pixel). Set by the Meta Pixel, a service provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Meta services (such as Facebook and Instagram) and to build audiences for our advertisements. When granted, information about your visit (such as pages viewed and actions taken) is shared with Meta, which may combine it with information it holds about you as described in Meta’s own privacy policy. These load only if you grant the “Marketing” category in our cookie banner.

You can change or withdraw your cookie consent at any time via the “Consent Preferences” link in the footer of the Platform. Withdrawing consent stops the relevant technologies from collecting further data about your activity on the Platform, and does not affect your ability to use the Platform.

What it does

## Built for the way a Nookal clinic runs

### Two-way Nookal sync

Patients book over WhatsApp; appointments land directly in Nookal. Reschedules and cancellations sync back. No double entry, no overbooking.

### Intake collected in the chat

New patients answer the intake questions inside the WhatsApp conversation, so the booking arrives in Nookal with the details attached, before anyone picks up a phone.

### 24/7 multilingual triage

Answers pricing, availability, and condition questions over WhatsApp, even when the clinic is closed.

### Reminders & no-show chasing

Reminders, follow-ups, and no-show chasing run themselves, so your hours go to patients, not the phone.

Claims automation is a separate Vascue product and connects to Cliniko first; Nookal is a front-desk integration today. Nookal clinics interested in claims can read the [claims overview](/claims).

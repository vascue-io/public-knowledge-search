## One clearer view of the work around care.

Give operations and department leaders a way to explore activity, find friction and ask better questions without losing sight of the records behind an answer.

### A dashboard shaped around your work

Choose the reporting window, arrange the charts your team uses and move from a summary to the underlying records where that chart supports it.

### See patterns across the patient journey

Use charts and tables to understand channel activity, service performance and where a handoff or queue needs attention.

### Ask a follow-up question

The AI assistant can work with permitted operational data, return a table or chart and let your team pin a useful chart to its dashboard.

### Keep judgment with your team

Access follows channel roles and permissions. Staff can inspect AI runs, verify important answers and review proposed actions before confirming them.

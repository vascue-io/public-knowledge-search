Product 01

## Your front desk, on WhatsApp, 24/7.

Handle patient enquiries, bookings and quotations in your patients' language, with updates to Cliniko, Nookal or Jane. Your team can review conversations and take over.

### 24/7 Multilingual Support

Answers patients on WhatsApp around the clock, in their own language.

### Smart Scheduling

Books, reschedules and cancels straight into Cliniko, Nookal, or Jane.

[Learn more](/cliniko)

### Instant Quotes

Gives accurate prices from your fee schedule the moment a patient asks.

### Referral & Image Reading

Reads referral letters, PDFs and scans to route each patient correctly.

### Batch Processing

Handles high message volume at once, so no patient waits in the queue.

### Staff Override & Control

Your team can step in, edit or take over any conversation instantly.

Product 03

## Prepare, check and track claims.

Support preparation, checking, status tracking and payment reconciliation, while your team reviews, approves and handles exceptions.

### Quotation & Claim Forms

Prepares insurer quotation and claim forms from approved uploads or connected source records.

[Learn more](/claims)

### Fee-code Mapping

Applies clinic-approved fee mappings and surfaces missing or unusual cases.

### Claim Submission & Tracking

Routes staff-authorized claims through configured channels and tracks the outcome.

[Learn more](/claims)

### Pre-auth Automation

Prepares and tracks pre-authorisation work with staff control.

### Shortfall Flagging

Flags coverage gaps and shortfalls before the patient is billed.

### Payment Reconciliation

Matches incoming payments and email remittances against claims to close the loop.

[Learn more](/claims)

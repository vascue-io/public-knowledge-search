About Vascue

# Built by the people who  
ran the front desk.

Vascue is an AI front desk for healthcare, made by founders with direct clinical and hospital experience, and a low tolerance for admin getting in the way of care.

![3D illustration of a clinic reception: a doctor walks past the front desk and phone while a patient waits on a sofa](/images/illustration-3d/about-story.webp)

Our story

## We started inside the problem

Our founders ran front desks and operations in clinics and hospitals, and watched skilled people spend their days on phone tag, quoting, and claim forms instead of patients.

Vascue is the system we wished we'd had: an AI front desk that carries the operational load from first message to insurance claim, without sending raw patient data where it shouldn't go.

Today that is two products on one connected system. The [AI Front Desk](/ai-front-desk) answers and books patients on WhatsApp and writes into practice-management systems such as [Cliniko](/cliniko). [Vascue Claims](/claims) prepares, checks, routes, and reconciles provider-side insurance claims while staff keep the final submit; it is rolling out through a design-partner programme, starting with physiotherapy clinics.

Vascue Limited is [ISO 27001 certified](/security), and the same privacy-first architecture already handles patient messaging for a [Hong Kong hospital radiology department](/customers/radiology-hong-kong).

What we believe

## The principles behind the product

![3D clipboard with an orange tick mark](/images/illustration-3d/about-value-1.webp)

### Built for clinical reality

We model how care actually runs, real intake, real pricing, real claims, not a generic chatbot bolted onto a clinic.

![3D stopwatch with an orange hand](/images/illustration-3d/about-value-2.webp)

### Time back to care

Every automation exists for one reason: to return minutes to staff and patients, where they matter most.

![3D hand holding floating chart and analytics icons](/images/illustration-3d/about-value-3.webp)

### Decisions from data

Every conversation becomes insight, so teams can see what slows them down and act on it.

![3D handshake shaped like a heart](/images/illustration-3d/about-value-4.webp)

### Trust by design

Patient data stays under your control. Privacy isn't a feature we added; it's the foundation we built on.

![3D illustration of a doctor consulting with a patient across a glass desk](/images/illustration-3d/about-people.webp)

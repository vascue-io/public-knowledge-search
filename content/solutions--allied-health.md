The problem

## Every service runs by its own rules.

A multi-service clinic is really several clinics under one roof. Each discipline has its own intake questions, session lengths, pricing, and rebate rules, and your front desk is expected to remember all of them at once.

Vascue carries that complexity. It greets every patient on the right path for their service, quotes the right price, and books into the right calendar.

The front desk is only the first half. Communication, booking, and claims run as one connected loop, so a rebate question in one discipline and a claim in another follow the same path. Our guide to [end-to-end patient journey automation](/blog/end-to-end-patient-journey-automation-allied-health) and the [comparison of AI medical receptionists for allied health](/blog/best-ai-medical-receptionist-allied-health) show what that looks like in practice.

What it does

## One system across the whole clinic

### Per-discipline intake and pricing

Each service has its own forms, session lengths, and price list. Vascue applies the right one automatically.

### Claims automation

Turns each visit into a coded, checked, ready-to-file claim from connected practice records, with staff review before configured payer routing.

### One inbox, every service

Physio, dietetics, psychology: one front desk, one place for staff to pick up.

### Syncs to your systems

Cliniko, Nookal, Jane, or the built-in Vascue calendar. Bookings land where you already work.

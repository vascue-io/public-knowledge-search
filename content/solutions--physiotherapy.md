The problem

## You trained to treat patients, not to run a front desk.

As a clinic grows, the phone and the inbox grow with it. Quotes, rebookings, referral letters, and claims pile up between sessions, and they rarely wait until you're free.

Vascue takes front-desk load off the table by answering and booking, and takes the claims load off with a separate product that prepares, files, tracks, and reconciles each claim.

The claims side starts from the Cliniko records you already keep. For clinics claiming from national or private insurers through Vascue's design-partner programme, [Vascue Claims](/claims) turns those records into source-linked drafts, runs deterministic checks, and leaves the submit with your staff. Our [claims automation guide](/blog/insurance-claim-automation-clinics-hospitals) and the [claims FAQ](/claims/faq) cover the detail.

What it does

## Built for the way a clinic actually runs

### Bookings that sync both ways

Patients book over WhatsApp; appointments land in Cliniko, Nookal, Jane, or your built-in Vascue calendar, no double entry.

### Claims automation

Turns each visit into a coded, checked, ready-to-file claim from connected practice records, with staff review and configured payer routes. Cliniko is the first design-partner path.

### 24/7 enquiries & triage

Multilingual answers to pricing, availability, and condition questions, even when the clinic is closed.

### Less admin, more treatment

Reminders, follow-ups, and no-show chasing run themselves, so your hours go to patients, not the phone.

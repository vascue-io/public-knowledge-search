# Vascue AI clinic-booking research pilot

> Vascue is researching how patient-authorized AI assistants may discover
> participating clinics and request confirmed appointments. This is not a live
> public booking service or an available API.

Canonical page: https://www.vascue.io/ai-agents

## Current status

- The hosted booking gateway is offline.
- Testing is limited to synthetic data and test calendars.
- Cliniko is the first intended research integration.
- Clinics may register interest without providing a PMS credential.
- No production connection is created from the interest form.

The proposed design uses patient authentication, explicit confirmation,
deny-by-default agent approval, clinic-controlled permissions, and minimum-data
responses. These controls remain subject to technical, privacy, security, and
provider review before any real-patient pilot.

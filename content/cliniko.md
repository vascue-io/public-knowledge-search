What it does

## Built for the way a Cliniko clinic runs

### Two-way Cliniko sync

Patients book over WhatsApp; appointments land directly in Cliniko. No double entry, no overbooking.

### Claims automation

Cliniko is the first connected claims source: each visit becomes a coded, checked, ready-to-file claim with staff review before it goes anywhere.

### 24/7 multilingual triage

Answers pricing, availability, and condition questions over WhatsApp, even when the clinic is closed.

### Reminders & no-show chasing

Reminders, follow-ups, and no-show chasing run themselves, so your hours go to patients, not the phone.

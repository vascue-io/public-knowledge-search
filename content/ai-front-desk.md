Common questions

## Straight answers about the AI front desk.

[See the hospital case study](/customers/radiology-hong-kong)

What is the Vascue AI front desk?

A WhatsApp-based front desk for clinics and hospital departments. It answers enquiries, reads referral letters, quotes from your own fee schedule, and books appointments around the clock, in the patient's language, then writes the booking into your practice-management system.

Which channels and systems does it connect to?

Patients message on WhatsApp, WeChat, or Messenger. Bookings sync two-way with Cliniko, Nookal, and Jane, or land in Vascue's built-in calendar.

Can staff take over a conversation?

Yes. Every conversation is visible in the staff inbox, flagged when it needs a human, and one toggle hands it to a person. The AI steps back without breaking the thread and can be switched back on at any time.

Is patient data sent to a third-party AI model?

Not in the raw. Patient data is handled in Vascue's own environment and direct identifiers are masked before any content reaches a language model. Vascue is ISO 27001 certified and does not sell data.

How long does it take to go live?

Days, not quarters. We review your services, prices, and booking rules, customise the agents, and go live, with your team able to step in from the first conversation.

Is it proven in production?

Yes. A private hospital radiology department in Hong Kong runs it today: 110,000+ messages handled, 99.2% of replies rated correct by staff, and about 60 seconds from referral photo to quote. The case study has the details.

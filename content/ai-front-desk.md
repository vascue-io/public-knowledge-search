Common questions

## Straight answers about Vascue Front Desk.

[See the hospital case study](/customers/radiology-hong-kong)

What is Vascue Front Desk?

A WhatsApp-based front desk for clinics and hospital departments. It answers enquiries, reads referral letters, quotes from your own fee schedule, and books appointments around the clock, in the patient's language, then writes the booking into your practice-management system.

Will it work with our existing systems?

Patients message on WhatsApp. Bookings sync two-way with Cliniko, Nookal and Jane, or use Vascue's built-in calendar. Mindbody, PracticeHub, HexaHealth, Halaxy, Clinicea, ClinicOffice, Semble, Plato Medical and JERA are coming soon. For other systems, ask us to confirm your integration requirements during the walkthrough.

Can staff take over a conversation?

Yes. Every conversation is visible in the staff inbox, flagged when it needs a human, and one toggle hands it to a person. The AI steps back without breaking the thread and can be switched back on at any time.

Is patient data sent to a third-party AI model?

Patient data is handled in Vascue's own environment. Direct identifiers are masked before content reaches a language model. Vascue Limited's information security management system is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022; the certificate sets out the scope. Vascue does not sell patient data.

How long does it take to go live?

The rollout depends on your workflows, connected systems and review requirements. We work with your team to understand the scope before confirming a launch plan, with staff review and takeover in place.

Is it proven in production?

Yes. A private hospital radiology department in Hong Kong runs it today: as of August 2026, 150,000+ messages handled, 99.5% of replies rated correct by staff, and about 60 seconds from referral photo to quote. The case study has the details.
